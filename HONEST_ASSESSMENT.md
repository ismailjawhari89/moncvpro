## 💡 What We Need to Fix (Priority Order)

### 🔴 Critical (Make or Break):

#### 1️⃣ Visual Hierarchy
**Problem:** Everything looks same importance
**Fix:** 
- Section titles: Bigger, bolder, colored
- Content: Proper font sizes (14pt → 11pt → 9pt)
- Spacing: More whitespace between sections

#### 2️⃣ Layout Polish
**Problem:** Looks like text dump
**Fix:**
- Proper columns with visual separation
- Section backgrounds (light gray boxes)
- Better alignment and padding

#### 3️⃣ Typography
**Problem:** Generic font usage
**Fix:**
- Title: Bold + Large (28pt)
- Section headers: Bold + Medium (16pt)
- Body: Regular + Small (10pt)
- Different weights for hierarchy

---
